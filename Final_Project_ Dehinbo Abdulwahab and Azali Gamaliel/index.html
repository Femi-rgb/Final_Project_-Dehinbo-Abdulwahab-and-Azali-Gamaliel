United Arab Emirates

Overview

The United Arab Emirates (UAE) is a nation in the Middle East known for its modern cities, rich culture, and rapidly growing economy.

Quick Facts

  • Capital: Abu Dhabi
  • Population: 9.9 million
  • Official Language: Arabic
  • Currency: UAE Dirham (AED)

Why the UAE Stands Out

Record-Breaking Landmarks

Home to the Burj Khalifa, the tallest building in the world.

Unique Landscapes

Golden deserts, modern skylines, and beautiful coastlines.

Rich Culture

A blend of tradition and innovation shaping daily life.

Top Places to Visit

Dubai

Luxury shopping, futuristic architecture, and vibrant nightlife.

Abu Dhabi

Grand Mosque, Louvre Abu Dhabi, and cultural experiences.

Sharjah

Known for museums, art heritage, and traditional markets.
