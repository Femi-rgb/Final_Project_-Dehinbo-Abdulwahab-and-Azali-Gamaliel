Culture of the UAE

Traditions & Identity

The UAE preserves a rich cultural heritage shaped by Bedouin traditions, hospitality, and Islamic values.

Key Cultural Elements

  • Arabic is deeply tied to national identity.
  • Hospitality plays a major role in daily life.
  • Camel racing and falconry remain traditional sports.
  • Dates and coffee are symbols of Emirati hospitality.

Cultural Highlights

Traditional Dress

Kandura and Abaya represent modesty and cultural pride.

Hospitality

Guests are treated with generosity and respect.

Falconry

A UNESCO-recognized cultural practice.
