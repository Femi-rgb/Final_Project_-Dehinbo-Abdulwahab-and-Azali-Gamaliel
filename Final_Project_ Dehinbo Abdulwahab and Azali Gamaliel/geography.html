Geography of the UAE

Overview

The UAE features deserts, mountains, coastlines, and rapidly growing cities.

Main Geographic Features

  • Arabian Desert covering much of the country.
  • Hajar Mountains in the east.
  • Coastlines along the Arabian Gulf and Gulf of Oman.
  • Artificial islands such as Palm Jumeirah.

Geographic Highlights

Desert Landscape

Golden dunes define the interior lands.

Coastlines

Pristine beaches meet rapid urban development.

Mountain Regions

The Hajar Mountains provide unique scenery.
